## Extension Name
ZFocus - Website Blocker & Focus Timer

## Short Description (max 132 characters)
Block distracting websites and boost your productivity with customizable time limits and smart scheduling.

## Detailed Description

ZFocus helps you stay focused by limiting time on distracting websites.

**Key Features:**

- **Smart Time Limits**: Set custom time limits for each website group (e.g., 5 minutes of social media per hour)
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Block sites only during work hours, with full access on evenings and weekends
- **Beautiful Timer Overlay**: See remaining time with a non-intrusive countdown on blocked sites
- **Progress Tracking**: View daily and weekly statistics on blocked attempts and time saved
- **Hard Lock Mode**: Prevent yourself from pausing during work hours for serious focus sessions
- **Multiple Languages**: Available in English, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, and Vietnamese
- **Dark Mode**: Beautiful dark and light themes that match your system preference

**How It Works:**

1. Add websites you want to limit (social media, streaming sites, forums, etc.)
2. Set your time limit (e.g., 10 minutes per hour)
3. Configure your schedule (work hours only, or all day)
4. ZFocus automatically tracks your time and blocks sites when you exceed your limit

**Privacy First:**

- Zero data collection - your browsing habits are completely private
- All data stored locally on your device
- Optional Chrome sync for settings across devices
- Open source - verify our privacy claims on GitHub

**Perfect For:**

- Students studying for exams
- Remote workers needing focus
- Anyone wanting to reduce social media usage
- Professionals improving productivity

**What's Included:**

- Popup with quick stats and pause controls
- Full settings dashboard
- Website group management
- Historical statistics (last 30 days)
- Import/export settings for backup

Free forever. No ads. No tracking.
