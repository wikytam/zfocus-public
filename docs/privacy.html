Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Single Purpose Declaration ZFocus is a productivity tool designed to help users manage their time by limiting access to distracting websites. This is its sole purpose.

Data Collection

ZFocus does NOT collect any personal data.

We believe in complete privacy. Here's what that means:

  • No browsing history is collected
  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics are sent to external servers
  • No third-party tracking scripts are included
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone

Data Storage

ZFocus stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Settings (chrome.storage.sync): Your blocked website lists, time limits, schedules, and preferences. This data can optionally sync across your Chrome browsers if you're signed into Chrome.
  • Statistics (chrome.storage.local): Daily statistics including blocked attempt counts and time paused. Historical stats are kept for 30 days. This data stays on your local device only.
  • Timer Data (chrome.storage.sync): Current timer states for active blocked sites.

All data remains on your device or within your personal Chrome sync account. No data is transmitted to ZFocus servers because we don't have any servers.

Permissions

ZFocus requires certain browser permissions to function:

  • tabs: To monitor which websites you visit and apply blocking rules
  • storage: To save your settings and statistics locally
  • alarms: To reset hourly time limits
  • activeTab: To determine which tab is currently active for "active tab only" counting

These permissions are used solely for the extension's core functionality and nothing else.

Third-Party Services

ZFocus does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

The extension may optionally include error monitoring (Sentry) in development builds to help developers fix bugs, but this is disabled in production releases.

Open Source

ZFocus is open source. You can review our code at any time to verify our privacy practices:

https://github.com/wikytam/z-focus

Data Deletion

You can delete all ZFocus data at any time:

  • Go to ZFocus Settings
  • Click "Delete All Data"
  • Confirm the deletion

Alternatively, uninstalling the extension will remove all stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through the extension's update notes.
